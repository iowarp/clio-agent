A subagent works on part of a task in its own conversation and reports back to the
agent that started it. The `delegation` plugin, which the default agent carries, gives
an agent three kinds: a copy of itself, the read-only `explore`, `planner` and `review`
roles, and forks of its own work.

The agent hands a child work that stands apart from what it is doing, or that would fill its context with material it needs only a report of. The agent decides when to delegate. You can also ask for it in your own words.

## A copy of the agent

When the agent delegates without naming another agent, the child is a copy of the delegating agent: the same plugins and the same model, unless the agent picks another model for it. A child of a domain agent keeps that domain's tools and skills.

## The explore, planner and review roles

The `delegation` plugin also brings three roles. Each runs with the delegating agent's plugins, read-only: any tool that would change something is refused, with the reason "This session is read-only".

| Role | What it does | What it returns |
|---|---|---|
| `explore` | Surveys files, data, task records, logs and sources. | Findings, each with where it came from, such as a path and line, a task, a log entry or a URL, and where it looked and found nothing. No proposals. |
| `planner` | Reads what it needs and designs a [workflow](/docs/flows/#workflows) for the assignment. | A workflow the delegating agent can start unchanged, and a few sentences on why these steps. |
| `review` | Checks work or a claim it did not do against the material it rests on. | A verdict: it holds, it holds with the caveats named, or it does not hold, with what was checked for each point. |

A read-only role cannot start subagents or workflows, and it does not ask you questions; it reports open choices in its result. The agent can also delegate to subagents its plugins define, or to any installed agent.

## What a child starts with

A child starts with only the task the agent writes for it. It does not see the parent conversation: not your messages, and not the agent's earlier work. The delegating agent is therefore asked to write a self-contained task, with the goal, the inputs and where they are, the constraints, and the report it wants back. [Inspect the assignment](/docs/tasks/subagents/#inspect-the-assignment) to read exactly what a child received.

A child is told that no person reads its conversation, to stay inside its assignment, and to end with a report the delegating agent can act on: what it found or did, the evidence, and what remains open. It does not receive the guidance for presenting results to you.

A child that is not read-only can still ask you a question. The question appears in the conversation that started the work, named after the child, and your answer goes back to the child.

## Forks

A fork is a copy of an agent's work so far, sent in another direction with a note
that says what to do differently, such as testing a competing explanation. The agent
decides when to fork. A fork runs on the same model as its source.

| Where | What the fork is | Read more |
|---|---|---|
| A conversation | A new conversation with a copy of the history, listed under its source in the left panel as **Forked by the agent**. The note is its first message. | [Forks the agent makes](/docs/branch-and-rewind/#forks-the-agent-makes) |
| A subagent | A copy of the child beside it under the same parent, named after it with `~1` added. The parent's wait covers both. | [Subagent controls](/docs/tasks/subagents/) |
| A workflow step | A copy of the step drawn beside it, `b~1` for step `b`. The steps that waited on the step read both answers. Workflow approval and growth limits apply. | [Fork a step](/docs/flows/#fork-a-step) |

**Forks per task** under Settings → **Execution & history** → **Subagents** limits how
many forks one task, or one conversation, may have. Zero means unlimited.

## See them work

Two recorded conversations started from a plain request that named no tool or role;
the agent chose to delegate:

- [A reading guide](/conversations/delegation/reading-guide/): ten `explore` children read a folder of 93 design documents, and the agent wrote the guide from their reports.
- [A skill review](/conversations/delegation/skill-review/): six `review` children checked every plugin's skills, and the agent merged their verdicts.

[A branched study](/docs/examples/branched-study/) shows copies of the agent playing a
study's games in parallel. [Real conversations](/docs/tasks/recorded-conversations/#delegation-and-forks)
walks through the recordings, including an agent fork.
