Alembic is CLIO's computational chemistry agent. It finds compounds in PubChem, builds
and edits structures, computes energies, frequencies and thermochemistry with xTB, MACE
or NWChem, computes X-ray absorption spectra with FDMNES, and shows structures in a 3D
viewer that you can click, mark and edit. Each request below was typed as a plain
question, with no tools or steps named. Everything ran on one Linux machine.

## Before you start

Choose the **Alembic** agent for the conversation. The chemistry connectors are in
**Settings**, under Chemistry: ASE (calculators), Burette (the 3D viewer and sketcher),
PubChem, FDMNES and Avogadro. When a request needs one that is off, CLIO shows a card
that turns it on. Programs that are not installed, such as NWChem, FDMNES or Avogadro,
are installed only after you agree.

## An imaginary mode you can watch

*"Take ethane in the eclipsed conformation, optimize it with MACE keeping that
conformation, and compute its frequencies."*

The optimized eclipsed structure has one imaginary frequency, 287.29i cm⁻¹. Asked to
*"Show me the spectrum and play the imaginary mode"*, the agent played the mode in the
viewer.

<Capture client:visible image={await captureImage(imaginaryMode)}
  alt="The 3D viewer playing mode_000.xyz, frame 10 of 20: ethane with its two methyl groups twisting against each other"
  caption="The imaginary mode, played in the viewer. The frame slider and Pause control the animation." />

With a rectangle drawn over one methyl group (the viewer's **Region** button), the
question was *"What is this motion?"*. The agent named the marked atoms, measured their
rotation from the animation frames in code (about ±13.9°, the other methyl turning the
opposite way) and called it the torsion that leads from the eclipsed form to the
staggered one.

Pushed along that mode and re-optimized without constraints, ethane became staggered,
with no imaginary frequency. The staggered form is 0.1078 eV (10.4 kJ/mol) lower, or
8.5 kJ/mol with zero-point energy, both at the same MACE level.

## A conformer you edit by hand

*"Build ethylene glycol and optimize it with MACE."* The optimization landed in the anti
conformer. In the viewer's **Edit** mode, picking the O–C–C–O atoms shows the dihedral,
−178°. Typing 60 and choosing **Apply** writes the edited structure as a new file and
reports it to the agent.

<Capture client:visible image={await captureImage(dihedralEdit)}
  alt="The 3D viewer in Edit mode with four atoms selected and the line dihedral O0–C1–C2–O3: -178.0°, a slider, a value box and an Apply button"
  caption="Edit mode: the four picked atoms and their dihedral, ready to be set." />

*"Re-optimize from this geometry and tell me which conformer is lower and by how
much."* The agent started from the edited file, re-optimized with frequencies and found
the gauche conformer lower by 5.95 kJ/mol (5.7 kJ/mol with zero-point energy).

For more than bond, angle and dihedral edits, the same structure can be opened in
Avogadro 2. Asked to open it there, the agent found Avogadro was not installed, asked
before installing it, then opened the conformer. The full Avogadro window runs inside
the conversation.

## A reaction energy across three methods

*"What is the Gibbs energy of CH4 + 2 O2 → CO2 + 2 H2O at 298 K? Start with xTB."*
The agent balanced the reaction, ran O2 as a triplet and said so, and summed the
per-species results in code. Then *"Now do it with MACE too, and show both on one
energy diagram"*, *"Confirm the two biggest disagreements with DFT in NWChem"* and
*"Redo the whole thing at 500 K"*.

| Method | ΔG at 298 K (kJ/mol) |
| --- | --- |
| GFN2-xTB | −1220.6 |
| MACE-OFF23 small | −898.0 |
| B3LYP/6-31G* (NWChem) | −624.9 |

The agent asked before installing NWChem, installed it in the workspace and ran DFT
on all four species. It reported that none of the three methods matches the tabulated
value it remembered (about −800 kJ/mol for gas-phase water), so this DFT level confirms
neither of the cheaper results. At 500 K it reused the optimized structures and
recomputed only the frequencies and thermochemistry; ΔG moved by less than 1 kJ/mol,
because the reaction has the same number of gas molecules on each side.

## XANES of magnetite

*"Get magnetite from the Materials Project and show me the structure."* The first time,
CLIO asked for a Materials Project API key in the FDMNES connector's card; the key is
typed into the card and never into the conversation. The agent chose the cubic Fd-3m
entry, the lowest above the convex hull, and opened it with its unit cell.

<Capture client:visible image={await captureImage(magnetiteCell)}
  alt="The 3D viewer showing Fe3O4 Fd-3m (mp-aaaabcoo): iron and oxygen atoms with the unit cell outlined"
  caption="Magnetite from the Materials Project, with its unit cell." />

*"Compute the Fe K-edge XANES for each inequivalent iron site."* From the structure the
agent identified the tetrahedral iron (Fe–O 1.92 Å) and the octahedral iron (2.06 Å)
and ran FDMNES once per site. The self-consistent potential did not converge for this
cell, so it ran without one and said that the spectra are therefore less reliable.
Asked *"Why is the pre-edge stronger for one of them?"*, it answered from the computed
pre-edge heights (0.30 for the tetrahedral site, 0.08 for the octahedral one) and the
site geometry: the tetrahedral site has no inversion centre. With one iron atom
selected in the viewer, *"Which site is this one, and what is its coordination?"* got
the octahedral site, six oxygens at 2.06 Å.
