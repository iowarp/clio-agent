This worked example shows how to review a complete report and presentation in
CLIO. For the general workflow and prompts to adapt to your own files, see
[Working with files](/docs/working-with-files/#create-a-report-or-presentation).

## A research briefing

The example uses saved analysis and figures from OPAL 1, a plant-growth research
dataset. Its three-page Word report and eleven-slide meeting deck include speaker
notes, an editable design table and a comparison chart. The report and slides
preserve unresolved treatment labels and an unexplained early break in a vendor
proxy, so the interpretation remains tied to the available evidence.

The initial drafts were revised after visual review. This recording shows review
and navigation of the saved results after those corrections.

## Reusable design references

These fictional reference files demonstrate document structure and layout. They
were built with CLIO's shipped document libraries and reviewed as PDF pages.

| Reference | Editable source | PDF | Runnable builder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Two-page evidence report | [Word](/media/document-reference/reference-report.docx) | [Report PDF](/media/document-reference/reference-report.pdf) | [Report script](/media/document-reference/create-reference-report.mjs) |
| Six-slide research briefing | [PowerPoint](/media/document-reference/reference-deck.pptx) | [Deck PDF](/media/document-reference/reference-deck.pdf) | [Deck script](/media/document-reference/create-reference-deck.mjs) |

Both use the same [fictional source summary](/media/document-reference/reference-source.json).
The builders run in the prepared JavaScript workspace, where `docx`, `pptxgenjs`
and image dependencies resolve. Pass a task-owned output directory as their first
argument. Adapt their composition to your evidence and audience.

The file-guide recordings revise these references. Their saved results are also
available: [revised Word report](/media/document-reference/revised-report.docx)
and [PDF](/media/document-reference/revised-report.pdf), plus the
[revised PowerPoint briefing](/media/document-reference/revised-deck.pptx) and
[PDF](/media/document-reference/revised-deck.pdf). The slide revision required a
formatting correction after overview review; the downloads contain that corrected
version with the original chart and editable tables.
