The most useful answer is one a person can question again. CLIO lets a person point to evidence in a view and send that context back to the agent.

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Select and reference">

Select an observation with a click, drag a box around several, or Ctrl-click to select a trajectory. Shift-click adds or removes observations. **Reference this** attaches the selected records and current filters to your next question.

<WidgetRecording name="clio-select-reference" title="Select observations. Ask when winds peaked." caption="A box selection near the Bahamas carries thirteen observations into the next question. Watch the question being typed and the real answer identifying both 160 kt peaks." timingNote="Recorded continuously at normal speed." pointerGuide />

### What this unlocks

Ask about a specific change or an unusual observation without describing where it is or copying its values. CLIO receives the data behind your selection.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Mark a visual region">

Choose **Capture labelled regions**, draw a box, and add a comment. **Add region to message** carries the image and marked area into your request.


<WidgetRecording name="clio-region-figure" title="Mark a region. Turn it into a figure." caption="Draw a box, add a comment, and open the attached image before asking for a PNG. The figure appears directly in the answer. Review it, ask for more detailed coastlines, then open the complete close-up with both peak UTC labels." timingNote="Actual agent work plays at 8× speed and typing at 2–3×; gestures and image opening play at normal speed." pointerGuide />

[Download the generated figure](/media/dorian-bahamas-figure.png) · [Download the hurricane observations used in these recordings](/datasets/atlantic-hurricanes-noaa.csv)

### What this unlocks

Point to a spatial detail and ask CLIO to explain it, compare it, or make a new image from it. The marked region gives your words a visible location.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
