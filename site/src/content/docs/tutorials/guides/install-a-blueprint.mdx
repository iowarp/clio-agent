An agent blueprint gives a session a domain agent: its instructions, its experts, and its tools. This tutorial installs one from the default marketplace, uses it, and then shows how to update and remove it.

<Steps>

1. Open the marketplace list.

   Open Settings and choose **Marketplaces & blueprints**, then open the **Marketplaces** tab. The default marketplace lists the blueprints it publishes, with a version for each, and a status that should read **Ready**.

2. Install a blueprint.

   Choose **Install** next to a blueprint, for example **EarthScope Skills**. The row changes to **Installed**, and the blueprint appears on the **Installed** tab.

3. Look at what you installed.

   On the **Installed** tab, open the actions menu for the blueprint and choose **View details**. The dialog shows its status, who can use it, its version, the services it connects to, and where it was installed from.

4. Start a session with it.

   Choose the plus button in the sidebar, then **New session**. Open the **Agent blueprint** list and choose the blueprint. After you create the session, its name shows next to the session title.

5. Check for updates.

   CLIO does not update installed blueprints by itself. On the **Installed** tab, choose **Check for update** from the actions menu when you want the latest version.

6. Remove it.

   From the same menu, choose **Remove** and confirm.

</Steps>

To add a different marketplace, such as one your lab maintains, choose **Add marketplace** on the **Marketplaces** tab. See [Agent blueprints](/docs/blueprints/).
