Paper, ink and verdigris

The visual language for CLIO's Notebook theme. A science agent's interface should feel like a well-kept lab notebook: warm paper to read on for hours, dark ink for what was written, and one oxidized-copper accent for what the agent is doing right now.

Principles

Paper first

Surfaces are warm and quiet so a long session of reading results, logs and plots does not tire the eye. Nothing is pure white or pure black.

One accent, one meaning

Verdigris marks the primary action and the agent's live activity. If everything is teal, nothing is.

Color carries science state

Supported, running, refuted and superseded have fixed colors in every theme, so a study tree reads the same in Light, Dark and Notebook.

Show, don't tell

Provenance and status appear as icons with detail on hover rather than boxes of explanatory text.

Color

Every color is a token. Components read tokens and never hex values. The page you are reading uses these exact tokens, so switching your system theme shows the other variant live.

Contrast

Measured WCAG ratios for the pairs that carry text. Every pair clears AA (4.5:1) for body text.

Science states

The four states of a hypothesis or study node in the science log. Each has a tint for the chip background and a text color for the label and icon. Refuted is a finding, not an error, so it uses brick and stays distinct from the danger red used for failures.

Typography

Three faces with three jobs. The serif is the agent's voice and long-form reading. The sans is the interface. The mono is for data, paths, IDs and code. All three are on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License.

Which opening word did best across the 60 games?

The fork testing H2 wins. Opening with a word that covers the five most common letters solved the puzzle in 3.9 guesses on average, against 4.4 for the baseline.

Rules

Do

Use one primary button per view. Everything else is secondary or ghost.

Don't

Fill a large surface with the accent. It is for actions and live activity only.

Do

Show a state as an icon chip and put the explanation in its hover detail.

Don't

Use danger red for a refuted hypothesis. A refutation is a result.

Shape and space

Radius

4 inline
6 chips
8 controls
12 panels

Lines

Borders are 1px in --border. Dividers inside a panel use the lighter --rule. No drop shadows on in-flow content. Popovers and menus get one soft shadow and sit on --raised.

Spacing scale

Interface voice

The words in the chrome are part of the theme. The agent writes in its own voice in Literata; the interface around it is short and plain.

Do

Sentence case, verb first: Add plugin, Fork session.

Don't

Title Case or exclamation marks: Plugin Added Successfully!

Do

Say what happened and what to do: DeltaAI login node didn't answer. Retry or check the tunnel.

Don't

Surface raw exceptions or apologize: Error: ConnectionResetError(104)

Tokens

The full block for gact-tui. Light values sit on [data-theme="notebook"]; the dark variant applies when the system prefers dark. Names follow the theme plan.
