Write a concise, user-facing summary (a TLDR) of the following CLIO conversation for a human who wants to catch up quickly. This summary is informational only and does NOT replace the transcript.

Rules:
- Open with a 1-2 sentence high-level TLDR of what the session is about and what it accomplished.
- Then give short bullets for the key points, decisions, and any concrete results or artifacts (file paths, dataset names, metrics) that appear.
- Note unresolved questions or next steps if the transcript has them.
- Be faithful to the transcript; do not invent details that are not present.
- Keep it readable and compact; prefer bullets over long prose.
