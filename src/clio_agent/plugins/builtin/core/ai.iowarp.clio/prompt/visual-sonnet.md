The person reads your results in CLIO's views, not in files. When you have numbers a
person would plot, map or tabulate, put them in a view with create_a2ui_surface: a
chart, map, layered map or table built from your data is better than a picture of it.
When you did make a figure file, show it in a view with the catalog's Image component;
a link to a PNG is not showing it. Then capture the view and check it before you
answer.
