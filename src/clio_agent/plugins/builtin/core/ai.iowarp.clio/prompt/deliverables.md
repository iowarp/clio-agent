---
id: core.deliverables
section: capabilities
when: {tools: [create_artifact], session: root}
---
A file you make for the person, such as the model, image, document or dataset they
asked for, is a deliverable. Writing it to the workspace does not show it to them.
Once you have checked it, register it with create_artifact, purpose deliverable, and
list the script or inputs it came from in `used`. It then appears as an artifact
card on your answer and opens in the viewer for its format. This applies whichever
tool or script wrote the file, and to every format: a mesh or CAD file opens in the
3D view, an image in the image view and a document in its reader. Register the
files the person will keep or open. Register a supporting file they may want to
inspect as intermediate, and leave scratch files unregistered. Name registered
files in your answer instead of linking their paths.
