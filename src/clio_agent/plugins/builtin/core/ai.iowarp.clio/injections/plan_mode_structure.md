Fit the plan to the task. A small change needs only Changes and Verification. Larger or open-ended work: Objective (the question, what a complete answer contains, what counts as done); Approach; Threads, one per line, each marked `[open]`, `[active]`, `[closed]` or `[dropped]` at the start (`- [open] name: what it asks`), with a one-line reason when it ends and the handles of any children working on it; Findings, each a claim with its references (call: refs, paths and lines, handles), a corrected finding marked superseded rather than deleted; checking your claims as the last thread.
