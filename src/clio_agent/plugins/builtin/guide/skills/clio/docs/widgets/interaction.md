# Human-agent interaction

Carry a selection or a visual region into the next question.

The most useful answer is one a person can question again. CLIO lets a person point to evidence in a view and send that context back to the agent.

CLIO can also ask **you** a question with images, charts, and input controls.
See [Answering questions](docs/questions.md) for visual questions and ways to respond.

**Select and reference**

Select an observation with a click, drag a box around several, or Ctrl-click to select a trajectory. Shift-click adds or removes observations. **Reference this** attaches the selected records and current filters to your next question.

*Recording on the site: Select observations. Ask when winds peaked..* A box selection near the Bahamas carries thirteen observations into the next question. Watch the question being typed and the real answer identifying both 160 kt peaks.

### What this unlocks

Ask about a specific change or an unusual observation without describing where it is or copying its values. CLIO receives the data behind your selection.
**Mark a visual region**

Choose **Capture labelled regions**, draw a box, and add a comment. **Add region to message** carries the image and marked area into your request.

*Recording on the site: Mark a region. Turn it into a figure..* Draw a box, add a comment, and open the attached image before asking for a PNG. The figure appears directly in the answer. Review it, ask for more detailed coastlines, then open the complete close-up with both peak UTC labels.

[Download the generated figure](https://clio.iowarp.ai/media/dorian-bahamas-figure.png)

[Download the hurricane observations used in these recordings](https://clio.iowarp.ai/datasets/atlantic-hurricanes-noaa.csv)

### What this unlocks

Point to a spatial detail and ask CLIO to explain it, compare it, or make a new image from it. The marked region gives your words a visible location.

## Try the controls

Selection, filtering, reference, and region-capture controls are available in the sample widgets throughout these pages, wherever the view supports them.
