---
name: research_methodology
title: Choose and State a Research Methodology
description: Match a research question to a method capable of answering it, and make the method's assumptions and limits explicit before results are collected.
---

Start from what the question actually asks — a mechanism, a comparison, a
prediction, a characterization — and choose a method that can produce evidence
for that specific claim, not just a familiar one. State the method as a
falsifiable procedure: what data or result would support the hypothesis, and
what would weaken or contradict it.

Name the method's own assumptions and scope: what population, material
system, loading regime, or conditions it applies to, and what it does not
cover. A method borrowed from an adjacent field or a different material system
needs justification before its validity transfers.

Separate the method from the specific tool or instrument used to execute it —
a methodology is the reasoning structure; abaqus, xct, or tensile_testing are
how a step in that structure gets carried out. Keep that distinction explicit
so the method survives a tool change.

Return the chosen method, its assumptions and scope, the falsification
criteria, and the specific specialists or skills needed to execute each step.
