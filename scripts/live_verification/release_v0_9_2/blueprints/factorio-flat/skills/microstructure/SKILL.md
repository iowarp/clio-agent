---
name: microstructure
title: Reason Over Microstructural State and Its Property Consequences
description: Describe a material's microstructure in the terms that actually predict behavior — phase fractions, grain morphology, texture, and defect population — rather than a qualitative label.
---

Describe microstructure with the quantities that predict behavior: phase
identity and volume fraction, grain size and morphology (equiaxed vs.
columnar), crystallographic texture, precipitate size/distribution, and
defect population (porosity, inclusions). A qualitative description
("fine-grained", "typical AM microstructure") is not sufficient to support a
property claim.

Connect microstructural state to the process that produced it and the
property it controls: columnar grains and texture from directional
solidification (common in additive manufacturing and casting) produce
anisotropic properties; a bimodal grain structure changes both strength and
fatigue behavior from a unimodal one of the same average size.

When the microstructure was measured rather than assumed, cite the
measurement (see `microstructure_characterization`) and its sampling
adequacy; when it is inferred from a processing route, state that it is
inferred and what would confirm it.

Return the microstructural state in quantitative terms, its process origin
or measurement evidence, and the properties it is expected to control.
