---
name: nickel_superalloys
title: Reason Over Nickel Superalloy Behavior
description: Apply the gamma-prime/gamma-double-prime strengthening framework and high-temperature service behavior common to the nickel superalloy family before narrowing to a specific grade.
---

Identify the strengthening phase family before reasoning about properties:
gamma-prime (Ni3Al, e.g. Inconel 718's competitor alloys, Rene, CMSX series)
versus gamma-double-prime (Ni3Nb, e.g. Inconel 718) versus solid-solution
strengthened grades (Hastelloy X, Inconel 625) — each has a different
precipitation kinetics and therefore a different heat-treatment sensitivity.

Treat high-temperature service behavior as central, not incidental: creep
resistance, oxidation/corrosion resistance, and microstructural stability
(phase coarsening, sigma-phase formation) under prolonged elevated
temperature are the reason this family is selected, and a room-temperature
property alone does not qualify a superalloy for its intended service.

State the precipitate morphology and size regime the property claim depends
on — strength from gamma-prime/gamma-double-prime is sensitive to precipitate
size (peak-aged vs. over-aged) in a way many other strengthening mechanisms
are not.

Return the strengthening-phase family and its heat-treatment sensitivity, the
service-temperature regime the claim applies to, and hand off to the specific
grade skill (for example `inconel_718`) for grade-specific properties.
