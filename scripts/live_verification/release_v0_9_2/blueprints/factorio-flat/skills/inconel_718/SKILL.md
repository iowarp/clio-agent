name: inconel_718
title: Reason Over Inconel 718-Specific Behavior
description: Apply Inconel 718's specific gamma-double-prime strengthening, heat-treatment schedule, and AM-specific behavior rather than generic nickel-superalloy reasoning.
---

Inconel 718 strengthens primarily through gamma-double-prime (Ni3Nb, body-
centered tetragonal), with gamma-prime (Ni3(Al,Ti)) as a secondary
contributor and delta phase (Ni3Nb, orthorhombic) as an equilibrium phase that
forms on over-aging or slow cooling — delta at grain boundaries can pin grain
size usefully or embrittle the material depending on its morphology and
amount. Name which phase a claim is about; "the strengthening phase" is
ambiguous for this alloy.

State the heat-treatment schedule precisely (solution anneal temperature/time,
single- or double-aging schedule, cooling rate) — 718's response to heat
treatment is schedule-sensitive, and a property claim is only valid for the
schedule that produced it.

For additively manufactured 718 specifically, expect as-built anisotropy from
columnar grain growth along the build direction, Laves-phase segregation from
rapid solidification (distinct from the delta phase seen in wrought material),
and a strong dependence of final properties on the post-build heat-treatment
route chosen to dissolve or control that segregation — do not apply wrought
718 handbook properties to AM 718 without checking this.

Return the phase state and heat-treatment schedule the claim depends on,
whether the material is wrought or AM, and the properties or behavior that
follow, with their evidence.
